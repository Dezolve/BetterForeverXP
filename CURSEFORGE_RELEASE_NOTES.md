# Better Forever XP 2.8.1

**New: Customize the information on your bar.** Under `/bfxp` → Text & Numbers, independently toggle level/faction, current/total progress, percentage, remaining XP/reputation, and bar counts such as `19 bars • 1 left`.

- All five fields are enabled by default. Customize as much or as little as you like; the layout adapts to your selections and bar width.
- Works with XP and watched Reputation, without changing the 20 segments, colors or detailed hover tooltips. Your existing text mode and number format still apply.

Thanks to VirtualString for the suggestion!
